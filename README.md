# Nomination Form Filler

Fills in the Great Eastern *Appointment / Change of Nominee(s) / Trustee(s)* form (NBZ-FNOMI-V07) from a customer's ePartner Policy Detail printout.

1. Load the Policy Detail printout (PDF, PNG or JPG), or fill in the details manually.
2. Review the details, and optionally upload an answer sheet (.txt) or a photo of an IC / notes.
3. Generate and download the filled PDF.

Everything runs in the browser. Customer files are read on your own computer and are never uploaded anywhere; only the PDF and OCR libraries are loaded from public CDNs.

To run it offline, download `index.html`, `core.js` and `template.js` into one folder and open `index.html`.
